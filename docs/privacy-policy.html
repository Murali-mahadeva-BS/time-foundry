Privacy Policy

Time Foundry Chrome Extension  ·  Last updated: May 3, 2026

Overview

Time Foundry is a Pomodoro timer and task manager that runs entirely within your browser. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Data Collection

Time Foundry does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The extension collects no information about you whatsoever.

Local Storage

All data created by Time Foundry — including tasks, timer state, session history, and settings — is stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in chrome.storage.local API. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to any server.

Permissions Used

  • storage – Saves tasks, settings, and session history locally on your device.
  • alarms – Runs the Pomodoro countdown timer reliably in the background.
  • notifications – Alerts you when a work session or break ends.
  • idle – Pauses the timer automatically when your computer is idle or locked.

Third Parties

Time Foundry does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks. No data is sold or transferred to any third party.
